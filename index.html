Practical questions

Do you cover the OSCE?

Sessions focus on the oral board exam. OSCE preparation is not included.

Are the cases from the real exam?

No. We write original cases using the ABA’s published content outline and public sample-question format. We never ask for content from an actual exam.

Is Hot Seat Anesthesia affiliated with the ABA?

No. We're an independent prep service. The rubric is our own; the ABA doesn't publish its scoring scale.

Who examines me?

One of our two academic anesthesiologists. We don't use current ABA examiners or current ABA question authors. ABA policy bars them from paid board review.

I didn't pass the oral boards. Can you help?

Yes. You start with one scored mock exam, then a written plan for your next practice based on what we heard in that session. We don't ask about the content of your previous exam.

Is the session confidential?

Sessions are recorded only with your consent. The recording goes only to you, and we delete it after 90 days. Please don't share real patient details or the content of any actual exam.

Do you guarantee I'll pass?

No. The session includes a timed mock exam, feedback on your answers, and a written practice plan.

I'm a program director or group leader. Can you work with our candidates?

Yes — see our page for programs and groups.
