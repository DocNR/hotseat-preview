Who we are

Hot Seat Anesthesia is an independent exam-prep service. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA). References to the ABA APPLIED Exam are descriptive only.

What you are buying

  • A founding session is one 60-minute live mock oral exam on Zoom, followed by a score sheet, the recording (if you consent), and a written action plan within 48 hours.
  • A six-session pack is six such sessions, scheduled across your prep window.
  • All stems and practice materials are original. They are not taken from, and do not reproduce, any actual ABA exam.

No guarantee of exam results

Sessions are practice and feedback. We do not promise or guarantee any exam result, and your outcome depends on many things outside our control.

Refunds and rescheduling

You can get a full refund, or one free reschedule, if you tell us at least 48 hours before your session. Email hello@hotseatanesthesia.com. Inside 48 hours, we'll do our best to help but can't promise a refund or reschedule.

Recording

We record a session only if you consent. The recording is shared only with you, used by us only to score your session and write your plan, and deleted after 90 days.

What not to share during a session

Please do not share identifiable patient information, or the content of any actual ABA exam (including anything you remember from a prior attempt). If either comes up, we will stop that line of discussion and remove it from our notes.

Educational service only

Sessions are education about exam format and presentation. They are not medical advice, credentialing, or a substitute for your own clinical judgment.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
