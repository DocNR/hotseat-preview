Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Hot Seat Anesthesia is a trade name of Wycor, LLC [TODO: confirm DBA filed]. In these pages, “we” and “us” mean Wycor, LLC, doing business as Hot Seat Anesthesia.

What we collect

  • When you request the sample case: your email, and whether you asked from the retake page.
  • When a program contacts us: name, email, role, organization, approximate candidate count, and your message.
  • When you book: the details you give in our scheduling intake. Payment details go to Stripe, not to us.
  • If you arrived through a referral or campaign link, a first-party cookie stores that referral code and campaign tags for up to 90 days so we know which links bring people to the site.
  • Aggregate site analytics through Plausible, which does not use cookies to track you across sites.

How we use it

  • To send you what you asked for, run your sessions, and follow up about them.
  • To understand which outreach is working. We don't sell your information.

Session recordings

Live sessions are recorded only with your consent. The recording is shared only with you and is deleted after 90 days. Please don't share identifiable patient information or the content of any actual ABA exam during a session; if either comes up, we stop that line of discussion and remove it from our notes.

Service providers

We use Stripe (payments), Cal.com (scheduling), Zoom (sessions), Supabase (form storage), Resend (email), and Plausible (analytics). Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Independence

Hot Seat Anesthesia is not affiliated with or endorsed by the American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA). We do not share your information with the ABA.

Your choices

Email hello@hotseatanesthesia.com to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, or to stop hearing from us.
